## A. Resolution Center reply (paste into ASC → version 3.0.0(61) → Resolution Center)

> Hello, and thank you for reviewing Autexa. Autexa is B2B business-management software used only by auto-service organizations (registered businesses — legal entities and sole proprietors) to run their own internal operations. It is not a consumer app and is not sold to individuals. Answers to your questions:
>
> **1. Who are the users that will use the paid content and services in the app?**
> Employees of an auto-service organization (a registered business). Within a business, the owner/director and staff (mechanics, cashiers, managers) use Autexa to run that company's own operations: work orders, cash register/receipts, parts inventory, payroll, staff scheduling, and the company's own clients and vehicles. Individuals do not use Autexa for personal purposes — every account belongs to a business.
>
> **2. Where can users purchase the content and services that can be accessed in the app?**
> Nowhere inside the app — the app sells nothing and contains no store, prices, or purchase flow. Autexa is provided to an organization under a business-to-business service agreement between our company and the business. A business contacts us to request access; we verify it is a real business and set up the organization's account manually. The organization is billed directly (by invoice / bank transfer to the legal entity) outside the app. Individual end users never purchase anything.
>
> **3. What specific types of previously purchased content and services can a user access in the app?**
> None in the sense of digital content — there is no content store, media library, or catalog. A signed-in employee accesses their own company's business-management tools and their own operational data (their work orders, inventory, payroll, clients). This is the organization's own data, made available because the employer has an active business service agreement with us — not previously purchased digital content.
>
> **4. What paid content, subscriptions, or features are unlocked within the app that do not use In-App Purchase?**
> None are unlocked by any purchase inside the app. The iOS app is login-only: no store, no price, no paywall, no purchase of any kind. Whether a company has an active service agreement is arranged entirely outside the app as a B2B contract invoiced to the business. The app does not unlock features in exchange for a payment made by the user; it simply reflects the access the organization already has under its business agreement with us.
>
> **5. Are the enterprise services in your app sold to single users, consumers, or for family use?**
> No. Autexa is sold only to organizations (registered businesses — legal entities and sole proprietors) for managing their own auto-service operations. It is not sold to single users, consumers, or for family/personal use. Access is provisioned per organization for that organization's employees. This is why the service is billed business-to-business, outside the app, and does not use In-App Purchase (Guideline 3.1.3(c), Enterprise Services).
>
> Our website (autexa.pw) reflects this: it does not show purchasable subscription prices and has no self-service individual sign-up. A business submits an access request that we review and provision manually; pricing is arranged individually by a sales manager, billed to the business by invoice.
>
